## The study method

For each new concept: define it in plain language → understand the formula and its assumptions → work the provided example → solve independent GATE-style questions → write down your mistake or shortcut → recall it without looking. In the app, only check a subject as mastered if you can explain it to another beginner.

- **First pass (days 1–56):** concept foundations and short daily quizzes.
- **Guided integration (days 57–70):** mixed-subject review and error logs.
- **Exam-mode revision (days 71–84):** timed mocks on selected days and targeted error repair.
- **Final taper (days 85–89):** last 100-mark mock, formula recall, and light review.
